About

I am a Ph.D. student in theoretical physics at the University of Tokyo. I also work as a junior research associate at RIKEN iTHEMS. My research interests include quantum chromodynamics, quantum field theory in curved spacetime, and related topics.

Research

QCD in curved spacetime

I study how the relevant degrees of freedom of QCD are affected by strong spacetime curvature.

Hadron physics

I am also interested in effective string descriptions of hadrons and their spectra in nontrivial backgrounds.
